# Working brand architecture

**SUPPORTING.** This document records a working master-brand model developed during BOBA Direct
planning. It does **not** amend [`VISION.md`](../VISION.md). VISION-1 remains the CURRENT why /
Non-Goals / GTM-outcome authority.

## Working master-brand model

```text
BOBA BEAR
│
├── FOOD
│     current commercial priority
│
├── WEAR
│     future
│
└── CULTURE
      emerging / soon
```

## Strategic interpretation

| Pillar | Working meaning |
|---|---|
| Food | taste / craving / recurring ritual |
| Wear | identity / self-expression |
| Culture | belonging / participation |

These pillars are expressions of **one brand world**. BOBA Bear should not become:

```text
restaurant
+ unrelated clothing store
+ generic culture page
```

## Working brand framing

Founder-supplied direction (existing brand language; **not** new canonical VISION authority):

```text
for the Unbothered
```

This pack records that framing so later product/UX work can stay consistent with it. Recording it
here does **not** insert it into VISION-1, did **not** create `D-368` (later allocated to Customer
Menu Read Projection Authority), and does not authorize implementation.

## Commercial implementation focus

**FOOD remains the only currently planned commercial implementation focus.**

Wear and Culture are brand-architecture and research material in this pack. They are **not**
implementation-authorized by this documentation.

VISION-1 already allows long-term expansion toward merchandise and drops without abandoning the
owned direct-order core ([`VISION.md`](../VISION.md) §9). That is not the same as authorizing Wear
or Culture commerce now. ROADMAP deferred capabilities and VISION Non-Goals still govern what may
be built.

## Drops relative to pillars

Food / Wear / Culture are **brand pillars**.

Drops are a **potential recurring campaign/release mechanism** that may span pillars. They are not
themselves a fourth commercial domain.

Working conceptual structure (not CURRENT architecture; **not** `D-368`, which is Customer Menu
Read Projection Authority):

```text
Brand Drop
├── Food Drop
├── Wear Drop
├── Culture activation
└── Collaboration
```

Authority boundary for that candidate (WORKING, not D-368):

A BrandDrop **MUST NOT** automatically become:

- pricing authority
- inventory authority
- checkout authority
- Food Menu authority
- Wear SKU authority
- Culture participation authority

Underlying domains remain authoritative. See [`ux-authority-principles.md`](./ux-authority-principles.md)
and [`working-decisions.md`](./working-decisions.md) EXP-WD-007 / EXP-WD-008.
